About Our School

We, in Colegio de San Pascual Baylon envision ourselves as a Christ-centered community, that integrates the Gospel, values inspired by St. Pascal Baylon and strive for the holistic formation of the person in the service of the Church and society.

Thus, we commit ourselves to the salvific mission of the Church through Christian Catholic Quality Education, the promotion of cultural traditions, and humble service to the Church in the building of a more humane and just society.
